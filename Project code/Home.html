Welcome to Ocean Awareness

Our oceans cover more than 70% of Earth’s surface and are home to millions of species. This website teaches you fascinating facts, ways to protect the ocean, and quizzes to test your knowledge.

Why Oceans Matter

  • The ocean produces over half of the world’s oxygen.
  • It regulates the Earth’s climate by absorbing heat.
  • Billions of people rely on the ocean for food and livelihoods.

Did You Know?

The deepest part of the ocean is the Mariana Trench, which goes down about 11,000 meters (36,000 feet)!
