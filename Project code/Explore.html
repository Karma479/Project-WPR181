Amazing Facts About Oceans

  • The ocean produces over 50% of the world’s oxygen.
  • More than 80% of the ocean remains unexplored.
  • The Great Barrier Reef is the largest living structure on Earth.
  • The longest mountain range on Earth is underwater: the Mid-Ocean Ridge.
  • More than 3 billion people depend on the ocean for food.
